I'm a **Computer Scientist** at the [Argonne Leadership Computing Facility](https://www.alcf.anl.gov) and **Adjunct Research Professor** at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://www.uic.edu), where I build visualization tools that turn petabyte-scale simulations on the world's fastest supercomputers into actionable scientific insight. Recent work has won **Best Paper at ISAV 2023 and 2024** and **1st place at the IEEE SciVis Contest 2023**.

My research lives at the intersection of **scientific visualization, human-computer interaction, and high-performance computing** &mdash; instrumenting in-situ libraries like [ASCENT](https://ascent.readthedocs.io) and [SENSEI](https://sensei-insitu.org) for exascale platforms, designing immersive XR interfaces for data exploration, and prototyping the next generation of human&ndash;AI collaboration tools. I serve as **General Chair of [IEEE VIS 2027](https://ieeevis.org)** in Chicago, founded [ChicagoCHI](https://www.chicagochi.org) (the SIGCHI local chapter), and hold patents from work at [Microsoft Research](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9818228B2) and [GN Hearing](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3796673B1).

Earlier projects include [SpiderSense](/news/sensing-the-environment-through-spidersense) (a wearable that lets you "feel" your surroundings, featured by Forbes and exhibited at Chicago's Museum of Science and Industry), contributions to [Pixar's USD](https://graphics.pixar.com/usd/docs/index.html) and [DreamWorks' Premo](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2614106.2614123), and [SAGE2](/news/sage2-a-new-approach-for-data-intensive-collaboration-using-scalable-resolution-shared-displays/) &mdash; a collaboration platform for large-display walls used at dozens of institutions worldwide.
